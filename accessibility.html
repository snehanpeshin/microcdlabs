Accessibility

MicroCD Labs Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Our commitment

MicroCD Labs aims to make this website usable by people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies. We use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, text alternatives for meaningful images, and responsive layouts where practical.

Target

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This is an ongoing effort and does not represent a certification of full conformance.

Known limitations

Some embedded or exported technical tools may have accessibility limitations, particularly interactive diagrams, canvas-based views, and complex modelling controls. We are working to improve labels, keyboard operation, instructions, and alternative explanations for those experiences.

Request assistance or report a barrier

If you have difficulty using the site or need information in another format, email info@microcdlabs.com. Please include the page, the problem encountered, and the format or assistance that would be useful.

Response approach

We will review accessibility feedback and make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information or address the barrier.
