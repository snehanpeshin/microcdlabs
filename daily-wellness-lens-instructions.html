Daily Wellness Lens

Instructions and Safety Information

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Intended Use

Daily Wellness Lens is a wellness trend app from Karigari Home LLC. It helps users view general wellness trend estimates from guided phone sensor checks and on-device baseline samples.

Daily Wellness Lens is not a regulated medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent disease. It does not replace professional medical care.

How to Use the App

  1. Open Daily Wellness Lens.
  2. Review the Today screen for the current wellness trend estimate.
  3. Tap Check to start a guided sensor check.
  4. Allow camera, microphone, and motion permissions if prompted.
  5. Follow the on-screen guidance and remain still during the check.
  6. Return to Today to review the updated wellness trend estimate.
  7. Open Signals for signal quality, saved check history, privacy notes, and safety information.
  8. Open Sources for citations and data-source explanations.

Permissions

The app may request camera access for guided pulse-style waveform checks, microphone access for breathing and voice feature checks, and motion access for steadiness checks. The current version does not connect to external health databases or care-plan services.

Safety Information

Do not use Daily Wellness Lens for emergency decisions, diagnosis, treatment, medication changes, disease management, or any medical decision. If you feel unwell, have symptoms, or have a medical concern, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For emergencies, call local emergency services.

Wellness scores can be affected by lighting, movement, microphone noise, device position, sleep, exercise, caffeine, stress, illness, and normal day-to-day variation. Treat results as general wellness trend information only.

Privacy

The app is designed to use derived feature values and wellness scores rather than raw audio or raw video recordings. See the privacy policy for current details.
