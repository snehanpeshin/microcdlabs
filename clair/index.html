CLAIR by MicroCD Labs

Better evidence for canine dental triage.

CLAIR is a saliva-based adjunct in development for use alongside the oral examination. The proposed workflow combines collection, controlled processing on a centrifugal microfluidic disc, and optical readout to support triage and the owner conversation.

Discuss a CLAIR pilot See the workflow

Development boundary: CLAIR is not for sale or clinical use. Analytical performance, workflow timing, and clinical utility remain to be established for each panel.

01 / Intended role

Support the examination.
Explain the next step.

A saliva-based adjunct for the conversation about further dental assessment.

CLAIR asks whether a quantitative saliva panel can add useful evidence alongside the awake oral exam. The first planned setting is general veterinary practices with active dental services.

Clinical judgment remains central. CLAIR does not replace indicated dental assessment, probing, or radiographs. A low result must not delay otherwise indicated care.

04 / Evidence before rollout

Three questions.
Three development gates.

Clinical added value remains unproven. Study size and timing depend on feasibility, a funded protocol, and statistical planning.

  1. 01

    Analytical readiness

    Can the panel measure reliably?

    Evaluate repeatability, interference, sample recovery, and collection and process controls. Report invalid samples.

  2. 02

    Independent clinical evaluation

    Does it add useful evidence?

    Compare the awake exam, panel alone, and exam plus panel against a blinded reference assessment using anesthetized probing and dental radiographs. Lock the panel and thresholds before independent validation and include realistic confounders.

  3. 03

    Customer validation

    Does it work for the clinic?

    Study workflow fit, owner understanding, willingness to pay, support needs, and repeat use with veterinary practices.
