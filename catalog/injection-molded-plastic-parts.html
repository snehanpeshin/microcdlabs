Available variants

  • Development options: concept review, CAD/DFM feedback, material selection, pilot tooling, and supplier shortlisting.
  • Production options: low-volume pilot run, bridge production, injection molding, cartridge assembly, and packaging support.
  • Documentation options: drawing package, material certificate, dimensional report, and supplier quality documentation by quote.

Specification checklist

  • Confirm intended application: RUO, teaching, prototype, OEM evaluation, or diagnostic development.
  • Confirm fluid compatibility, pressure/flow range, operating temperature, and cleaning method.
  • Share expected annual volume, target cost, regulatory context, material constraints, CAD status, and tolerance needs.
  • Confirm whether the request is for prototype, pilot, bridge production, or supplier transition.
