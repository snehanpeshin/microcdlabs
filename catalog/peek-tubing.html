Available variants

  • Diameter options: common microfluidic OD/ID combinations including 1/32 in., 1/16 in., 1/8 in., and metric microbore sizes.
  • Material options: PTFE, FEP, PEEK, silicone, and low-adsorption or biocompatible grades where available.
  • Pack options: trial length, roll, cut-to-length kit, or tubing assortment for setup development.

Specification checklist

  • Confirm intended application: RUO, teaching, prototype, OEM evaluation, or diagnostic development.
  • Confirm fluid compatibility, pressure/flow range, operating temperature, and cleaning method.
  • Specify tubing OD/ID, fitting thread type, wetted material, connector standard, and maximum pressure.
  • Check dead volume, chemical compatibility, bubble tolerance, and available space around the chip or instrument.
