Available variants

  • Format options: sterile/non-sterile, filtered/non-filtered, low-retention, DNase/RNase-free, skirted/non-skirted, or automation-ready.
  • Packaging options: bulk, racked, refill, bagged, case quantity, individually wrapped, or cleanroom-compatible where available.

Specification checklist

  • Confirm intended application: RUO, teaching, prototype, OEM evaluation, or diagnostic development.
  • Confirm fluid compatibility, pressure/flow range, operating temperature, and cleaning method.
  • Confirm sterility, purity claims, volume, plate geometry, temperature compatibility, automation fit, and pack size.
  • Check certification needs such as DNase/RNase-free, pyrogen-free, PCR clean, or cell-culture treated.
