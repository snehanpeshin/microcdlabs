Available variants

  • Material options: PDMS, glass, COC, PMMA, or hybrid formats depending on chemistry and optical needs.
  • Porting options: inlet/outlet count, luer adapters, NanoPort-style fittings, punched PDMS ports, or bonded manifolds.
  • Channel options: straight channels, mixers, droplet junctions, gradients, chambers, traps, or assay-specific geometry.

Specification checklist

  • Confirm intended application: RUO, teaching, prototype, OEM evaluation, or diagnostic development.
  • Confirm fluid compatibility, pressure/flow range, operating temperature, and cleaning method.
  • Provide channel drawing, target dimensions, material preference, port layout, and bonding/sterility needs.
  • Confirm optical readout needs such as brightfield, fluorescence, microscopy, or imaging through the device.
