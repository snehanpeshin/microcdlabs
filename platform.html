Product review

Recommended improvements before broader release.

The current alpha already supports simple geometry, sketches, cuts, holes, fillets, coordinate editing, and export. The next phase should make the workflow easier for new users while adding microfluidic design intelligence.

Send feedback
01 Guided first-use flow

Start users with a short chip setup: units, substrate size, channel depth, fabrication method, and target application.

02 Touch-first navigation

Improve iPhone, iPad, and trackpad use with explicit rotate, pan, zoom, fit, and reset controls that do not require hidden shortcuts.

03 Clearer tool modes

Separate sketch, feature, view, and inspection modes so users always know whether they are drawing, cutting, selecting, or moving the camera.

04 Dimensioned editing

Add click-to-edit dimensions, constraints, centerlines, grids, snapping presets, and visible units for channel and cartridge features.
