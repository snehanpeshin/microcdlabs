Before you bring your team.

Is LabOps an FDA-certified quality system?

No. Its FDA-grounded templates support documentation and review; they do not establish regulatory compliance or FDA certification. Intended-use assurance, procedures, retention, backup/restore, and applicable electronic-record controls require assessment.

Does it file regulatory reports or release products automatically?

No. LabOps records assessments, evidence, and review decisions. Qualified people remain responsible for reportability, regulatory submissions, and authorized product release.

Does it connect automatically to Modeler and Analyzer?

Use Send to LabOps in Modeler or Analyzer to download a snapshot and open LabOps. Choose the experiment, review the snapshot, and confirm the upload.

How do I start?

Open the workspace if you already have access, or discuss your team size, workflow priorities, and intended use with MicroCD Labs. Confirm access and commercial terms before adoption.
