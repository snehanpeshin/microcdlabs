MicroCD Labs Microfluidic Modeler

Build early cartridge, channel, cut, port, and enclosure concepts in a browser-based workspace.

Sketch and shape Blocks, rectangles, circles, slots, sketch cuts, and coordinate editing.
Modify features Extruded cuts, holes, fillets, mirror, duplicate, and linear patterns.
Export concepts Use GLB export for concept sharing and early geometry review.

Install like an app

You can add the Microfluidic Modeler to your iPhone, iPad, or Mac home screen for faster access. It will open as a focused app-style workspace.

iPhone or iPad Open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Mac Use Safari Add to Dock or Chrome Install when available.

Before launching

This alpha modeler is for research, concept design, education, and early feasibility work. Designs are not validated for clinical use, manufacturing release, regulatory submission, or patient-facing decisions. Review all geometry, materials, tolerances, and assay requirements with qualified engineers before fabrication or use.
