01 / Engineering support

Microfluidics & diagnostic cartridges

The application or manual assay needs a practical fluidic architecture and disposable workflow.

Concrete deliverables
Requirements brief, functional block diagram, cartridge workflow, disc sequence map, and prototype roadmap.
Useful inputs
Intended application, non-confidential assay steps, sample and reagent constraints, interfaces, and current evidence.
Review criteria & handoff
Workflow completeness, stated interfaces, and documented assumptions; handoff includes the agreed architecture or development brief.

02 / Engineering support

Mechanical design & prototyping

A concept needs mechanical definition, interfaces, fixtures, or a fabrication handoff.

Concrete deliverables
Concept CAD, interface drawings, fixture requirements, vendor questions, and a build-and-test plan.
Useful inputs
Existing sketches or CAD, critical interfaces, material constraints, fabrication route, and the decision the prototype must support.
Review criteria & handoff
Interface dimensions, drawing/CAD scope, and open fabrication questions; handoff includes the agreed files and review notes.
Discuss CAD or a prototype ↗

03 / Engineering support

Verification planning & technical review

Requirements, test methods, evidence, and development decisions need a coherent plan.

Concrete deliverables
Draft verification matrix, test outline, evidence-gap memo, analysis plan, or technical development roadmap.
Useful inputs
Current requirements, available data, known risks, intended use, and the next development decision.
Review criteria & handoff
Requirement coverage, proposed acceptance criteria, and evidence gaps; handoff includes the agreed test/review matrix.
Discuss a review or test plan ↗

04 / Engineering support

Supplier & manufacturing coordination

The team needs clearer specifications and technical communication with external suppliers.

Concrete deliverables
Supplier shortlist, comparison matrix, RFQ package, bill of materials, and handoff questions.
Useful inputs
Specifications, drawings if available, quantities or scale assumptions, supplier constraints, and documentation needs.
Review criteria & handoff
Specification completeness, comparison criteria, and supplier responsibilities; handoff includes the agreed RFQ/comparison package.
Discuss supplier support ↗

Deliverables are examples, not a bundled promise. Fabrication routes and supplier responsibilities are defined per project; no in-house fabrication, certification, or regulatory approval is implied.

Engagement process

Four steps from question to handoff.

  1. 01

    Initial discussion

    Share non-confidential context, evidence, constraints, and the decision ahead.

  2. 02

    Scoped proposal

    Define work, assumptions, responsibilities, timing, and fees.

  3. 03

    Agreed deliverables

    Confirm outputs and review criteria before paid work begins.

  4. 04

    Review & handoff

    Review the work product, document open questions, and agree next steps.
