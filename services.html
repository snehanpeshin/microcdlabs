Microfluidic product development

Problem: The application is understood, but the fluidic architecture, interfaces, and development path are not.

MicroCD Labs provides

Requirements framing, fluidic architecture, component and material questions, interface definition, risk review, and prototype planning.

Typical deliverables

Requirements brief, system block diagram, component shortlist, design-review memo, or prototype roadmap.

For startups, research teams, and OEM groups moving from concept toward feasibility.

Diagnostic cartridge and consumable development

MicroCD Labs provides

Consumable requirements, workflow map, cartridge concept, design questions, supplier brief, or test plan.

Lab-on-disc development

Problem: A centrifugal assay concept needs clearer fluidic functions, timing, actuation logic, and experimental evidence.

MicroCD Labs provides

Disc workflow mapping, chamber and channel functions, spin-sequence questions, assay timing, sample transfer logic, and experiment planning.

Typical deliverables

Disc functional architecture, sequence map, design assumptions, experimental matrix, or prototype coordination package.

For teams exploring centrifugal microfluidics, sample preparation, or integrated diagnostic workflows.
