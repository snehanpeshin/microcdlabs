Selection answer

How should you choose?

Choose a chip material around the fluid, optical method, channel geometry, required volume, and development stage. PDMS is practical for early soft-lithography work; glass supports optical and solvent-intensive workflows; COC and PMMA can suit cartridge-oriented development when the manufacturing path is considered early.
