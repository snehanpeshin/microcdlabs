Selection answer

How should you choose?

Choose a chip material around the fluid, optical method, channel geometry, required volume, and development stage. PDMS is practical for early soft-lithography work; glass supports optical and solvent-intensive workflows; COC and PMMA can suit cartridge-oriented development when the manufacturing path is considered early.

General selection guidance · Updated November 6, 2026. These are general selection considerations, not verified specifications for a listed item.

Before requesting a quote

Confirm the complete system requirement

Define requirements first. Source, documentation, pricing, availability and lead time require confirmation in the proposal.

Material and chemistry

Review solvent exposure, adsorption, autofluorescence, gas permeability, and bonding method.

Geometry and interfaces

Define channel dimensions, ports, dead volume, pressure, and compatible tubing or fittings.

Prototype to production

Match the chip process to the current experiment and the likely scale-up route.
