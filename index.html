MicroCD Labs / Flagship in development

CLAIR / Human + veterinary oral health

Earlier recognition.
Better-informed oral care.

CLAIR explores saliva testing alongside professional assessment for people and animals. Our clinical question is whether additional evidence can help appropriate assessment, treatment, and follow-up happen sooner.

Human oral health Veterinary oral health →
Shared engineering, separate evidence

The human program begins with a periodontal assessment research question. The veterinary program focuses on canine dental triage. Each needs independent validation.

CLAIR is a concept platform in development. Not for sale or clinical use. Performance has not been established.

01 / The clinical question

What changes
after a concern is recognized?

A useful result must lead to a clinically appropriate next step.

The care pathway connects recognition to professional assessment, treatment when indicated, and ongoing care. We will study clinical benefit, patient or animal welfare, decision-making, and cost alongside test accuracy. Human and veterinary programs require separate evidence.
