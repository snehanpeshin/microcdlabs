CLAIR / Human + veterinary oral health

Saliva-testing technology
for oral-health research.

CLAIR is an in-development platform combining a disposable centrifugal microfluidic disc with controlled optical readout. Two programs explore how saliva measurements could support professional oral-health assessment.

01 / Proposed platform workflow

From saliva collection to optical measurement.

  1. 01

    Collection

    Define saliva collection and sample-validity checks.

  2. 02

    Sample handling

    Develop controlled centrifugal processing on a disposable disc.

  3. 03

    Assay reaction

    Evaluate reagents, controls, and reaction conditions.

  4. 04

    Optical acquisition

    Develop controlled illumination and image capture.

  5. 05

    Analysis

    Study endpoint and time-dependent signals against reference methods.

Proposed workflow · Functional diagram, not final cartridge geometry, hardware, timing, or performance.

Veterinary oral health
