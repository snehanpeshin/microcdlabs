MicroCD Labs / Flagship in development

CLAIR · Canine oral-health triage

Better evidence for canine dental triage.

CLAIR is a saliva-based adjunct in development to support the oral examination and conversations about further dental assessment. Lab-on-disc centrifugal microfluidics brings controlled sample handling and optical readout into one proposed point-of-care workflow.

Explore CLAIR Discuss a clinic pilot →
First application, focused development

Designed for general veterinary practices with active dental services. Proposed benefits, workflow fit, and willingness to pay require validation with clinical teams.

CLAIR is a concept platform in development. Not for sale or clinical use. Performance has not been established.

01 / The clinical question

A clearer next step.
A better-informed conversation.

The awake oral exam starts the conversation. CLAIR asks whether quantitative saliva evidence can help veterinary teams explain why further dental assessment may matter.

Our first focus is canine oral-health triage in general practices with active dental services. Clinical added value remains to be established.

03 / A focused first application

Multiple signals.
One clinical question.

Can a combined panel add useful evidence alongside the oral examination?

Candidate measurements cover tissue activity, inflammation, oral chemistry, and bleeding, with collection and process controls built into the development plan.
