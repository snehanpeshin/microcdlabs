CLAIR by MicroCD Labs

Making saliva more reliable for quantitative point-of-care research.

CLAIR combines a disposable centrifugal assay disc with controlled smartphone imaging to reduce variability across sample preparation, assay timing, and readout.

Explore CLAIR Discuss a CLAIR pilot

Concept platform in development. Not for sale or clinical use. Performance has not been established.

The product thesis

A simple collection method needs a controlled measurement system.

Saliva collection is accessible, but dilution, debris, viscosity, manual transfer, timing, and phone imaging can make quantitative results difficult to reproduce. CLAIR is being developed to bring those variables into one defined workflow.

One controlled workflow

The disc prepares. The reader standardizes. The assay informs.

  1. 01
    Disposable assay disc

    Designed to meter, separate, mix, and time panel-specific chemistry.

  2. 02
    Reusable optical reader

    Designed to fix phone position, illumination, and image-acquisition conditions.

  3. 03
    Panel-specific interpretation

    Connects fluidic control with kinetic and endpoint measurements for a defined veterinary use case.

Intended use sequence

Four steps from oral swab to structured readout.

The first planned application focuses on supervised veterinary oral-health research.

  1. 1Collect

    Obtain a saliva sample with an oral swab.

  2. 2Load

    Transfer the sample into the disposable disc.

  3. 3Process

    Run controlled rotational fluid-handling steps.

  4. 4Read

    Capture standardized smartphone images.

Initial development focus

Canine oral-health decision-support research.

The planned first panel explores complementary dimensions of inflammation, tissue activity, oral chemistry, and bleeding using candidate markers such as LDH, MPO, phosphorus or pH, and occult blood.

Candidate panel composition, thresholds, analytical performance, and clinical interpretation require validation.
