CLAIR · Canine oral-health triage

Better evidence for canine dental triage.

CLAIR is a saliva-based adjunct in development to support the oral examination and conversations about further dental assessment. Lab-on-disc centrifugal microfluidics brings controlled sample handling and optical readout into one proposed point-of-care workflow.

First application, focused development

Designed for general veterinary practices with active dental services. Proposed benefits, workflow fit, and willingness to pay require validation with clinical teams.

CLAIR is a concept platform in development. Not for sale or clinical use. Performance has not been established.

CLAIR · Our flagship application

A clearer basis for the next dental-care conversation.

The proposed role is to add quantitative saliva evidence alongside the awake oral examination, helping veterinary teams explain why further assessment may matter. Clinical added value remains unproven.

The system

The disc prepares. The reader standardizes. The assay informs.

One defined path connects oral collection, centrifugal processing, controlled imaging, and structured interpretation.

Initial focus

A focused panel, with sample validity built in.

The candidate panel combines LDH, MPO, phosphorus, and pH with an occult-blood signal and collection and process controls. The development question is whether the combined panel adds useful evidence beyond the oral exam and existing tests.

Candidate markers, thresholds, analytical performance, and clinical interpretation require validation. A low result must not override otherwise indicated dental care.
